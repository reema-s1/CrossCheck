# NimbusCare+ Protection Plan

NimbusCare+ is an optional protection plan that extends coverage to two years from the date of purchase for phones, watches, and earbuds, and to three years for the Nimbus Laptop Air. It must be purchased within 60 days of buying the device.

NimbusCare+ adds coverage for up to two incidents of accidental damage every 12 months. Each accidental damage incident is subject to a service fee: $29 for screen-only damage on the Nimbus Phone X2 and $99 for other accidental damage on the phone. For the Nimbus Laptop Air, the service fee is $99 for screen damage and $299 for other damage.

NimbusCare+ also includes priority 24/7 phone support and express replacement shipping. NimbusCare+ costs $149 for the Nimbus Phone X2, $79 for the Nimbus Watch S, $29 for Nimbus Buds Pro, and $249 for the Nimbus Laptop Air. NimbusCare+ can be cancelled at any time for a prorated refund. NimbusCare+ does not cover loss or theft.
