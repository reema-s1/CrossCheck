# Erasing and Factory Resetting a Device

Before selling, trading in, or sending a device for service, you should back it up and erase it. On a Nimbus Phone X2, go to Settings > General > Transfer or Reset > Erase All Content and Settings. You will need your device passcode and Nimbus Account password.

Erasing the device turns off Find My Nimbus and Activation Lock, removes your Nimbus Account, and deletes all data. Nimbus devices use hardware encryption, so erasing destroys the encryption key and makes data unrecoverable.

If you cannot remember your passcode, you can put the phone into Recovery Mode by connecting it to a computer with the Nimbus Link app and holding the side button and volume down button for 10 seconds. Recovery Mode erases all data. If Activation Lock is on, you will need the Nimbus Account password to set the device up again.
