# Nimbus Limited Warranty

Every new Nimbus device (Nimbus Phone X2, Nimbus Watch S, Nimbus Buds Pro, and Nimbus Laptop Air) includes a one-year limited warranty starting on the original date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship, including faulty buttons, dead pixels on the display, and batteries that fail to hold a charge due to a defect.

The limited warranty does not cover accidental damage such as cracked screens, liquid damage, cosmetic scratches, or damage caused by third-party accessories. It also does not cover devices that have been opened or repaired by an unauthorized service provider.

To make a warranty claim, customers need the device serial number and proof of purchase. Warranty repairs are free of charge. If a device cannot be repaired, Nimbus will replace it with a new or refurbished device of equivalent functionality. Replacement devices are covered for the remainder of the original warranty or 90 days, whichever is longer.
