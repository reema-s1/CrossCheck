# Your Privacy During Repairs

Nimbus technicians do not need access to your personal data to perform most repairs. When you send a device for service, you can turn on Repair Mode, which keeps your data encrypted and locked while still allowing the technician to run diagnostics. Repair Mode is available on Nimbus Phone X2 and Nimbus Laptop Air running NimbusOS 5.2 or later.

If Repair Mode is not available, Nimbus recommends backing up and erasing your device before service. Nimbus never asks for your Nimbus Account password during a repair, and technicians are prohibited from viewing photos, messages, or other personal content.

Diagnostic logs collected during service contain hardware information only and are deleted 90 days after the repair is complete. Repair records, including the device serial number and repair description, are kept for 3 years for warranty purposes.
