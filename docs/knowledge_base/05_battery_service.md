# Battery Service and Battery Health

Nimbus batteries are designed to retain at least 80% of their original capacity after 800 complete charge cycles on the Nimbus Phone X2 and 1000 cycles on the Nimbus Laptop Air.

You can check battery health in Settings > Battery > Battery Health. If the maximum capacity drops below 80% while the device is under warranty or NimbusCare+, the battery is replaced for free.

Out of warranty, a battery replacement costs $89 for the Nimbus Phone X2, $79 for the Nimbus Watch S, $49 per earbud for Nimbus Buds Pro, and $199 for the Nimbus Laptop Air.

To extend battery life, Nimbus recommends enabling Optimized Charging, which pauses charging at 80% and finishes charging shortly before you usually unplug. Avoid keeping devices in temperatures above 35°C (95°F).
