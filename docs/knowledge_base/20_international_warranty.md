# International Warranty Coverage

The Nimbus limited warranty for the Nimbus Laptop Air and Nimbus Watch S is international: service is available in any country where Nimbus or its Authorized Service Providers sell that product.

The Nimbus Phone X2 warranty is country-specific because phone models differ by region. Phones can only be serviced in the country or region where they were originally purchased. For example, a Nimbus Phone X2 bought in Canada can be serviced in Canada or the United States, but not in the United Kingdom.

NimbusCare+ coverage follows the same rules as the limited warranty for each product. Some service options, such as express replacement, may not be available in every country. Import taxes or shipping fees may apply when service is requested outside the original country of purchase.
