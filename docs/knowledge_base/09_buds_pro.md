# Nimbus Buds Pro

Nimbus Buds Pro are wireless earbuds with active noise cancellation (ANC) and a Transparency mode. Each earbud gives up to 6 hours of listening time with ANC on, or 8 hours with ANC off. The charging case provides up to 30 hours of total listening time. Five minutes in the case gives about 1 hour of listening.

The case charges via USB-C or any Qi-certified wireless charger. Nimbus Buds Pro come with silicone ear tips in four sizes: extra small, small, medium, and large. An Ear Tip Fit Test is available in the Nimbus app.

Nimbus Buds Pro work with any Bluetooth device, but automatic device switching, spatial audio with head tracking, and firmware updates require a Nimbus Phone or Nimbus Laptop Air. A lost single earbud can be replaced for $69 out of warranty.
