# Nimbus Phone X2 Technical Specifications

The Nimbus Phone X2 has a 6.3-inch OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and a peak brightness of 2000 nits. It is powered by the Nimbus N3 chip and comes with 8 GB of RAM. Storage options are 128 GB, 256 GB, and 512 GB.

The rear camera system includes a 50-megapixel main camera and a 12-megapixel ultra-wide camera. The front camera is 12 megapixels. The phone supports 5G, Wi-Fi 7, and Bluetooth 5.4.

The battery capacity is 4,500 mAh. The phone supports 30 W wired fast charging via USB-C, which charges the battery to 50% in about 25 minutes, and 15 W wireless charging. It weighs 187 grams. The Nimbus Phone X2 launched in March 2025 in three colors: Graphite, Glacier, and Sage. It will receive software updates for at least 6 years from launch.
