# Nimbus Trade In

Nimbus Trade In lets you exchange an eligible device for credit toward a new purchase or for a Nimbus Gift Card. Eligible devices include Nimbus phones, watches, and laptops from the last five years. Nimbus Buds Pro are not eligible for trade-in credit but can be recycled for free.

The trade-in value depends on the model, storage, and condition. The device must power on, hold a charge, and have no cracks on the display or back. Devices that do not qualify for credit are recycled for free.

For online trade-ins, Nimbus sends a prepaid trade-in kit. You have 14 days to send your device after receiving the kit. If the device's condition does not match your description, Nimbus will send a revised offer, which you can accept or decline; if you decline, the device is returned to you for free. Turn off Find My Nimbus and erase the device before sending it.
