# Screen Repair

If your Nimbus Phone X2 screen is cracked or shows lines, flicker, or touch problems, you can request a screen repair online, at a Nimbus retail store, or through an Authorized Nimbus Service Provider.

Without NimbusCare+, an out-of-warranty screen replacement for the Nimbus Phone X2 costs $279. A Nimbus Watch S screen replacement costs $199. A Nimbus Laptop Air display replacement costs $549. With NimbusCare+, the screen-only service fee for the phone is $29.

Most in-store phone screen repairs are completed the same day, usually within 3 hours. Mail-in repairs take 5 to 7 business days including shipping. Only genuine Nimbus displays are used. After a screen repair, the True Color calibration is restored automatically the next time the phone connects to Wi-Fi.
