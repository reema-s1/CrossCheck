# Backing Up and Restoring Your Device

Nimbus Cloud Backup automatically backs up your phone each day when it is connected to power, locked, and on Wi-Fi. Every Nimbus Account includes 5 GB of free cloud storage. Nimbus Cloud+ storage plans are available with 50 GB for $0.99 per month, 200 GB for $2.99 per month, and 2 TB for $9.99 per month.

Backups include app data, device settings, photos (if Nimbus Photos sync is off), messages, and home screen layout. Backups do not include data already stored in Nimbus Cloud, such as contacts, calendars, and synced photos.

You can also back up to a computer using the Nimbus Link app with a USB-C cable. Local backups can be encrypted with a password; encrypted backups also include saved passwords and health data. To restore, select "Restore from Nimbus Cloud Backup" or "Restore from Computer" during device setup.
