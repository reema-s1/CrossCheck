# Nimbus Laptop Air Overview

The Nimbus Laptop Air has a 13.6-inch display with a resolution of 2560 by 1664 pixels and 500 nits of brightness. It uses the Nimbus N3 Pro chip and a fanless design, so it runs silently. Configurations are available with 16 GB or 24 GB of memory and 512 GB or 1 TB of storage.

The Laptop Air offers up to 18 hours of video playback on a single charge. It has two USB-C ports that support charging and data transfer, a headphone jack, and a MagCharge magnetic charging port. It supports up to two external displays when the lid is closed.

The Nimbus Laptop Air weighs 1.24 kg. Because it is fanless, under very heavy sustained workloads the chip may reduce its speed to keep the temperature safe. This behavior is normal and does not indicate a defect.
