# Shipping and Delivery

Standard shipping is free on all orders from the Nimbus online store and usually arrives in 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping costs $12 and arrives in 1 to 2 business days. Same-day courier delivery is available in select cities for $15 on orders placed before noon.

Nimbus ships to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and most EU countries. Nimbus does not ship to P.O. boxes for orders over $1,000. A signature is required for all deliveries of phones and laptops.

You can track your order on the Order Status page. If a package is marked delivered but you have not received it, wait 24 hours and then contact Nimbus Support. You can also choose free in-store pickup at a Nimbus retail store, usually available within 2 hours.
