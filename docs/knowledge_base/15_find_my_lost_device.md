# Find My Nimbus and Lost or Stolen Devices

Find My Nimbus helps you locate, lock, or erase a lost device. You can use it at find.nimbus.example or in the Find My app on another Nimbus device. Find My Nimbus can locate a phone for up to 24 hours after its battery runs out, using the low-power reserve.

Lost Mode locks your device with a passcode, displays a custom message with a contact phone number, and suspends Nimbus Pay. If you believe your device was stolen, report it to local law enforcement and your carrier.

Nimbus cannot track, locate, or unlock a lost device on your behalf, and Nimbus Support cannot remove Activation Lock without proof of purchase. Neither the limited warranty nor NimbusCare+ covers lost or stolen devices. Nimbus Buds Pro can be located when within Bluetooth range and will play a sound to help you find them.
