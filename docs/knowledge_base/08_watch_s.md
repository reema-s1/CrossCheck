# Nimbus Watch S Overview

The Nimbus Watch S is available in 41 mm and 45 mm case sizes, in aluminum or titanium. It has an always-on OLED display and a battery life of up to 36 hours with typical use, or up to 72 hours in Low Power Mode.

Health features include heart rate monitoring, blood oxygen measurement, sleep tracking, and fall detection. The ECG feature is available only in the United States and the European Union. The Watch S does not measure blood pressure or blood glucose.

The watch charges with the included magnetic fast charger, reaching 80% in about 45 minutes. It requires a Nimbus Phone running NimbusOS 5 or later; it is not compatible with other brands of phones. Cellular models let you make calls and use data without your phone nearby, which requires a carrier plan.
