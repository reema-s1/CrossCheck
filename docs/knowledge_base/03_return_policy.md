# Return Policy

Nimbus products bought directly from the Nimbus online store or a Nimbus retail store can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. The device must be in its original condition, with all included accessories and the original packaging.

Opened software, gift cards, and personalized (engraved) products cannot be returned. Earbuds can only be returned if the hygiene seal has not been broken, unless they are defective.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds typically take 5 to 10 business days to appear. Return shipping is free for customers in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. Products bought from third-party retailers must be returned to that retailer according to its own policy.

Holiday purchases made between November 1 and December 25 can be returned until January 8 of the following year.
