# Water and Dust Resistance

The Nimbus Phone X2 is rated IP68 under the IEC 60529 standard, and is tested to a maximum depth of 6 meters for up to 30 minutes. The Nimbus Watch S is water resistant to 50 meters under ISO 22810 and can be used for shallow-water swimming, but not for scuba diving or high-velocity water sports. Nimbus Buds Pro earbuds are rated IPX4 for sweat and splash resistance; the charging case is not water resistant.

Water resistance is not permanent and can decrease as a result of normal wear. Liquid damage is not covered by the limited warranty. Liquid damage is covered as accidental damage under NimbusCare+.

If your phone gets wet, do not charge it until it is completely dry, which may take up to 5 hours. Do not use a hair dryer or insert cotton swabs into the charging port.
