# Troubleshooting: Device Feels Warm or Overheats

It is normal for Nimbus devices to feel warm while charging, during setup, while restoring from a backup, or during gaming and video recording.

If your Nimbus Phone X2 gets too hot, it may display a temperature warning and temporarily limit features: charging may pause, the display may dim, and the camera flash may be disabled. Move the device to a cooler place out of direct sunlight and remove any thick case.

If a Nimbus Laptop Air feels hot, make sure it is on a hard, flat surface, quit apps that are using a lot of processing power in Activity Monitor, and update to the latest version of NimbusOS. If the device repeatedly shows a temperature warning in normal room conditions, run Nimbus Diagnostics and contact Nimbus Support. Do not place devices in a refrigerator or freezer to cool them down.
