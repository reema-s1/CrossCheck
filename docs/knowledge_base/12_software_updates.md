# Updating NimbusOS

NimbusOS updates include new features, bug fixes, and security patches. To update a phone, go to Settings > General > Software Update. To update a Nimbus Laptop Air, open System Settings > General > Software Update.

Before updating, back up your device and make sure it has at least 50% battery or is connected to power. Updates require at least 6 GB of free storage.

You can turn on Automatic Updates to install updates overnight while your device is charging and connected to Wi-Fi. Security Responses are small, rapid security fixes that can be installed between regular updates and may require a restart.

Nimbus Watch S updates install from the Nimbus Watch app on the paired phone; the watch must be on its charger and at least 50% charged. Nimbus does not support downgrading to an earlier version of NimbusOS.
